---
title: "Aashiyana"
date: 2026-11-13
categories: Kalaam
tags: Urdu
---


Kisi der shaam daftar se thaki haari  
Wo jab mere paas aa kar baithti hai  
ungliyon se meri hatheli dhoondhti hui  
sar mere kandhe par rakh kar kehti hai  
"Ab laga jaise ghar aayin hoon"  
Main chup reh kar muskura deta hoon  
Shayad yun ki is khamoshi mein sukoon hai  
Uske bagal mein baithne se, paas hone mein  
Tasalli hai, itminan hai, jeene ki wajah hai  
Ya phir shayad isliye chup reh jaata hoon  
Kyun ki mujhe nahi pata ki use kaise bataun  
Ki jab tak usne mujhe nahi dhoondha tha  
Aur nahi apnaya tha, aur gale lagaya tha  
Usse pehle main bas dar-o-deewar tha  
Uska aashiyana banne se pehle  
Bikhra sa, veeran sa khandahar tha
