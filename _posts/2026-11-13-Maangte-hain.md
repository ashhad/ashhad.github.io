---
title: "Maangte hain"
date: 2026-11-13
categories: Kalaam
tags: Urdu
---


Muhabbat ke aage wo mehboob maangte hain  
Maangne waale bhi Kya khoob maangte hain  

Hai yaqeen ki muhabbat mein wafa khushnaseebi hai  
Hum aseer-e-husn phir bhi us bewafa ko maangte hain  

Wujood, ana, Hasti sab bhula di thi pehli nazar mein  
Dekhte hain ab sayyad maqtuul se kya maangte hain  

Jis khuda ne duniya ki aazmaishen taqdeer mein likh di  
Goya Usi khuda se taqdeer badalne ki dua maangte hain
