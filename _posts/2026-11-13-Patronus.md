Whenever the night gets heavy  
And all the troubles weigh me down  
Their fangs at my throat, dragging me down  
I sit back and take a deep breath  
And try to remember the last time I saw you  
The faint picture of you in my head  
Captured by my thieving eyes  
And in an instant the grip starts to loosen  
As the melancholy of your pain comes riding  
Like a patronus charm but quite the opposite  
The pit in my stomach gets deeper, chest heavier  
Eyes burning with all the tears that won't shed  
The pain changes form into a nascent desire  
Clouds of what ifs and if onlys start flowing around  
there is a cloud burst and I get swept away by my  
And while I deal with this new warm feeling in my chest  
Warm enough to burn me down if overexposed  
I have forgotten about all the other worries of the world  
Your longing has robbed others pains of their feeling  
As I use you once again to cheat the world, cheat myself
