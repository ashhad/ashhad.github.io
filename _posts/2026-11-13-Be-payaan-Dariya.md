---
title: "Be-payaan Dariya"
date: 2026-11-13
categories: Kalaam
tags: Urdu
---


Jab saare shor sharaabe khatam ho jaate hain  
Raat sheher ki raushniyo ko nigal jaati hai  
Main apni thakan le kar, apne vasvase samet kar  
Baith jaata hoon kabhi sahil par kabhi kisi chhat par  
Kabhi kisi pahaad ke neeche behti nadi ke saath  
Aur dekhta rehta hoon, sunta rehta hoon  
Dariya ke be-payaan aanchal ko  
Chaand ke jalwa angez noor ko  
Nadi ki athkheliyon ke alhad shor ko  
Aur yaad karta hoon tumhe  
Ki kaise us be-payaan dariya ki tarah  
Main tumhe dekhta rehta tha  
Bina kisi aur khyaal ke, bina uktaaye, bina thake  
Aur jaise har lehar dar lehar dariya badalta hai  
Waise tumhare chehre mein dhoondh leta tha  
Kuch naya dekhne ko, sanwaarne ko, sahejne ko  
Kaise Maine dekha hai chaand ko tum bante hue  
Kabhi poora chehra, kabhi sirf tumhare nath bane  
Wo Noor wo chehra wo aankhein wo thandak  
Maine tumhe utna dekha jitna chaand ko dekha chakor ne  
Aksar jab akele mein tumhare voice note sunta hoon  
To khaali kamre mein tumhari aawaz aise bhar jaati hai  
Jaise raat ke andhere mein kisi pahad ke tale  
Koi be-kal behti nadi Raat ke shor ko cheer rahi ho  
us rawaani se bhare shor se bhar leta hoon khud ko  
Ki uski jhankar se kuch din aur kat jaayenge  
Aur phir yun kabhi kisi chaandni raat mein sahil kinare  
Hum ek din mar jaayenge
