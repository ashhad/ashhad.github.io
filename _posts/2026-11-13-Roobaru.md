---
title: "Roobaru"
date: 2026-11-13
categories: Kalaam
tags: Urdu
---


Wo mere roobaru baitha ho  
Aur Darmiyan ki saari dooriyan  
Haath bhar ki doori mein simat kar reh jaaye  

Damakta hua chehra muskurati hui aankhe  
Meri chor nazrein hatt he na rahi ho  
Husn ka kohsar mere saamne  
Aur main ek adna sa maghloob musafir  
main chhone ke liye haath badhaun  
Wo palkein jhuka kar muskura de  
Uske baalon mein haath pherte he  
Kaanpte haath, be-rawani dhadakta dil  
Aur duniya wahin khatam ho jaaye  

Use dekh dekh kar ye sochun  
Mujhe is khoobsurat chehre se pyaar hai  
Ya ye chehra mere ishq se dilkash hai
