---
title: "Haar gaye tum"
date: 2026-11-13
categories: Kalaam
tags: Urdu
---


Le de kar ek ummeed he bachi thi khasaare mein  
Kya Ghazab hai ki wo ummeed bhi haar gaye tum  

Ye mehfiley ye kahkahe ya dostiyon ke saraab  
Yaani ki apne akelepan se bhi haar gaye tum  

Tumhare haathon par to lakeere thi uske naam ki  
Kaho kya bataoge ki phir kaise haar gaye tum  

Do hasti aankhe ek raushan soorat aur meetha lehja  
Bas itni si baat par apna sab kuch haar gaye tum  

ye aalishan ghar aur iske khaali kamre batate hain  
ki bohat kuch jeet kar bhi shayad sab haar gaye tum
